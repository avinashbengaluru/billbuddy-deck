Cloud Architecture

How BillBuddy & Tally Work Together

BillBuddy sits in the middle — managing WhatsApp, PhonePe payments, and sales clerks while automatically syncing with Tally behind the scenes.

BillBuddy Web & Mobile Hub — Tally stays online or offline without interrupting sales

Resilient Offline Queueing

If the Tally computer goes offline or crashes, BillBuddy continues taking orders without interruption. All sales queue locally and push to Tally automatically when reconnected.

Structured Data Layer

From XML Exports to Query-Ready Database

Tally stores transactions as unformatted XML blobs (`CASH SALES, WALK IN`). BillBuddy structures customer names, dental item shades, and batch expiry dates into SQL rows.

Unlocks Advanced Capabilities:

  • ✓Real-time inventory search by variant shade & batch.
  • ✓Instant customer purchase history & reorder alerts.
  • ✓WhatsApp API integration for automated receipts & tracking.
